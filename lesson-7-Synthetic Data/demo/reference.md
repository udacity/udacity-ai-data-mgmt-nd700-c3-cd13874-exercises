## Inventory Management

Inventory management ensures the store has the right products available at the right time.

### Stock Keeping Unit (SKU)

A SKU is a unique identifier assigned to each product variant in a store's inventory system. For example, different sizes or colors of the same product will typically have separate SKUs.

Example product entry:
SKU: TSHIRT-BLK-M-10293

### Inventory Counts

Retail stores perform inventory counts to verify that system records match physical inventory.

Types of counts include:

- **Cycle counts** – Regular counts of selected items
- **Full inventory counts** – Complete store inventory audits
- **Spot checks** – Quick verification of specific products

Inventory discrepancies can occur due to theft, damaged goods, or scanning errors.

## Returns and Exchanges

Retail stores typically allow customers to return or exchange products under specific conditions.

Common return policies include:

- Returns allowed within a certain number of days
- Items must be unused and in original packaging
- Proof of purchase is required

Refunds may be issued as:

- Original payment method
- Store credit
- Product exchange

Certain items such as clearance products or personal care items may be non-returnable.
